# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the work and respectful of the person.
- Give specific, evidence-based feedback.
- Accept correction and correct the record when you are wrong.
- Respect differing constraints, contexts, and levels of experience.
- Do not publish private information, credentials, or unreleased product material.
- Do not use the project to harass, demean, or exclude others.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualised language or attention.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Presenting fabricated evidence, screenshots, or audit results as real.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate.

Report incidents privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile or, where available, GitHub's private reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as possible.
